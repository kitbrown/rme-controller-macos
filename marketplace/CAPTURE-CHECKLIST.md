# Chris’s capture checklist

Install the v1.1.8.0 Marketplace candidate and use the current working profile. Its runtime is the accepted v1.1.7 build with SDK 3 manifest metadata. Capture lossless screenshots and original camera files; compose final gallery images at 1920 × 960 PNG. Use a landscape camera position, sharp focus and readable screens. Hide unrelated windows and personal information. Keep actual control labels and values intact.

## Three required gallery captures

1. **Controls at hand** — real Stream Deck + with the final Main, Core Audio, Mic 1 and TV/SPDIF controls visible (use the working profile’s pages as needed). Include the touch strip and physical dials. Take normal and muted versions with the same framing. Template: `templates/gallery-01-controls.svg`.
2. **Feedback in both directions** — TotalMix FX plus Stream Deck + in the same shot, or a real screen capture paired with a real device photo from the same state. Show matching target/level and Main Mute; capture Mono in both STEREO and MONO states. Template: `templates/gallery-02-feedback.svg`.
3. **Setup for your signal flow** — actual Stream Deck Property Inspector for Output Volume or Submix Fader, showing the preset and OSC settings. Include a second focused crop of mute/Mono/snapshot active-state examples if it improves legibility. Template: `templates/gallery-03-setup.svg`.

Replace every outlined capture area and delete the amber template warning before export. Do not upload template previews. Preserve image proportions; never stretch a device or invent live values. If a UI screenshot is too small to read, take a tighter capture rather than enlarging it heavily. Review the final exports at half size.

## Required hardware demo — one 60–90 second take or simple edit

- 0–10 s: show the Fireface UCX II, Stream Deck + and TotalMix FX; identify plugin version 1.1.8.0.
- 10–20 s: show Global OSC enabled and the matching local ports, then the configured Property Inspector.
- 20–40 s: turn an output dial and a source-to-output submix dial; show the corresponding TotalMix fader moving. Change a level in TotalMix and show the device updating.
- 40–55 s: press a dial to mute, show red MUTED feedback, then unmute. Show Main Mute and Mono changing both on the device and in TotalMix.
- 55–75 s: show Dim, a safe snapshot recall and its feedback, and momentary Talkback + Dim press/release. Only demonstrate audible Talkback if the current routing supports it; do not imply audio routing was verified otherwise.
- Final seconds: return to the normal working state. Do not toggle 48V merely for filming.

Export 1920 × 1080 MP4, preferably H.264, 30 fps. Export under 50 MB: the live Maker Console enforces a 50 MB upload limit, despite the published gallery guideline allowing under 250 MB. Keep clicks and resulting feedback visible together; no simulated hardware footage. Use no music unless you have rights. The demo can be an additional gallery item; three stills plus the demo makes four items. Save a 1920 × 960 PNG cover. Suggested filename: `hardware-demo-1.1.8.mp4`.
