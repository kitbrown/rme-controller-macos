Control RME TotalMix FX from Stream Deck on macOS. Use Stream Deck + dials to adjust hardware outputs and source-to-output submix levels, press to mute, and follow changes made in TotalMix with bidirectional Global OSC feedback.

Keys provide Main Mute, Dim, Mono, input/output mute, momentary Talkback + Dim, linked Analog 1/2 phantom power and snapshots 1–8. Presets cover Core Audio playback, Mic 1, TV/SPDIF, Main, SPDIF output and Phones. Custom OSC paths support advanced setups. Clear state labels and source colors show what is active or muted.

Requires macOS 13 or later, Stream Deck software 7.1 or later, and an RME interface with a Global OSC-capable TotalMix FX release. Dial actions require Stream Deck +. Tested with Fireface UCX II and TotalMix FX 2.10 beta 4; other interfaces may need custom mappings. Enable Global OSC; local defaults are 127.0.0.1, TotalMix receive port 7008 and plugin feedback port 9008. Check connected equipment before enabling 48V.
